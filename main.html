The Atelier

Slow craft, patient fire.

Every Auréa bracelet begins as raw ingot. It passes through eleven pairs of hands before it ever meets yours — drawn, hammered, polished, set.

We make no more than forty pieces a month. Each is numbered, signed, and accompanied by a hand-stitched leather case.
